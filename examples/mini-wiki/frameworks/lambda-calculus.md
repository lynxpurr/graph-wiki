# Lambda Calculus

Lambda calculus is a formal system for expressing computation based on
function abstraction and application. Introduced by Alonzo Church in the
1930s, it is equivalent in power to [[alan-turing]]'s Turing machine and
forms the theoretical basis of functional programming.

The close relationship between [[recursion]] and the Y combinator, and
between lambda calculus and [[computational-complexity]],
make it a cornerstone of theoretical [[algorithms|computer science]].
