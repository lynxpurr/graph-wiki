# Graph Theory

Graph theory is the mathematical study of networks composed of vertices (nodes)
and edges. Originating with Leonhard Euler's 1736 solution to the
Königsberg bridge problem, it now underpins modern [[algorithms]] for
network analysis, routing, and social graph computation.

Graphs generalize trees: every tree is a graph, but not every graph is
tree-like. [[breadth-first-search]] is a core traversal algorithm.
[[donald-knuth]]'s work includes extensive contributions to graph algorithms.
