# Recursion

Recursion is a problem-solving technique where a function calls itself on
smaller subproblems until reaching a base case. It is a fundamental concept
in [[algorithms]] and is closely related to mathematical induction.

Recursion underpins many [[sorting]] algorithms (like [[quicksort]] and
[[merge-sort]]), tree traversals, and the theoretical
foundations of [[lambda-calculus]].
