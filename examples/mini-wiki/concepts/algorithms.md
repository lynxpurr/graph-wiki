# Algorithms

An algorithm is a finite sequence of well-defined instructions for solving a
class of problems or performing a computation. The study of algorithms is
central to [[computational-complexity]] and relies heavily on appropriate
[[data-structures]].

[[donald-knuth]] systematized and popularized rigorous analysis of algorithms
in _The Art of Computer Programming_. Common design paradigms include
[[recursion]], divide-and-conquer methods such as [[quicksort]] and
[[merge-sort]], and dynamic programming.
