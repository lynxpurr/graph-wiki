# Big-O Notation

Big-O notation is Bachmann-Landau notation for an asymptotic upper bound. It
describes how resource use grows within [[computational-complexity]] analysis.

The bound can characterize best-case, average-case, or worst-case behavior,
depending on what is being analyzed. It is fundamental to comparing
[[algorithms]] independently of machine-specific timing.
