# Data Structures

A data structure is a specialized format for organizing, processing, and
storing data. The choice of data structure often determines the efficiency
of an [[algorithms|algorithm]].

[[donald-knuth]]'s _The Art of Computer Programming_ provides a comprehensive
treatment of fundamental structures including [[linked-list|linked lists]],
[[tree-structure|trees]], and [[hashing|hash tables]].
