# Sorting

Sorting is the process of arranging elements in a defined order. It is one of
the most studied problems in [[algorithms]] and [[data-structures]].

Classic sorting algorithms include [[quicksort]] (divide-and-conquer) and
[[merge-sort]] (stable merging). Performance is analyzed using
[[big-o-notation]] within the framework of [[computational-complexity]].
[[donald-knuth]] devotes an entire volume of TAOCP to sorting and searching.
