# Donald Knuth

Donald Ervin Knuth (born 1938) is an American computer scientist and
mathematician, best known as the author of _The Art of Computer Programming_
(TAOCP). He systematized and popularized rigorous analysis of [[algorithms]]
and [[computational-complexity]], using [[big-o-notation]] to characterize
algorithmic performance.

Knuth's work encompasses [[sorting]], [[hashing]], [[data-structures]],
and [[recursion]]. He is also the creator of the TeX typesetting system
and the concept of literate programming.
