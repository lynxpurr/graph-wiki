# Alan Turing

Alan Mathison Turing (1912–1954) was an English mathematician and logician
whose work laid the foundations of [[algorithms|computer science]]. His 1936
paper introduced the Turing machine, a formal model of computation that
underpins [[computational-complexity|complexity theory]].

Turing's work on the Entscheidungsproblem paralleled [[lambda-calculus|Church's
lambda calculus]], establishing the Church–Turing thesis. His contributions
extend to cryptography, [[algorithms|algorithmic]] theory, and the philosophy
of artificial intelligence.
