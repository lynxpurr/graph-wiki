# Alonzo Church

Alonzo Church (1903–1995) was an American mathematician and logician who
introduced the [[lambda-calculus|lambda calculus]] in the 1930s as a formal
system for expressing computation through function abstraction and
application.

Church's work on the Entscheidungsproblem produced the Church–Turing thesis,
the claim that every effectively computable function is computable by a Turing
machine — a result he reached independently of [[alan-turing|Alan Turing]]. His
formalism underpins [[recursion|recursive function]] theory and the foundations
of [[computational-complexity|computability]].
